# 📝 How to Use NotebookLM for PSOE Study Guide

> Step-by-step guide to accomplishing the "Build a PSOE Certification Study Guide" course using NotebookLM.

---

## 🎯 Course Goal

This course is **not about learning PSOE content directly**.  
It teaches you how to **use NotebookLM as an AI study assistant** to build your own personalized study guide for the PSOE certification.

By the end of the course, you should have:
- A NotebookLM notebook dedicated to PSOE preparation
- Your own study materials organized and structured
- A usable, interactive study guide to support revision

---

## Step 1: Create Your Notebook

- Open NotebookLM  
- Create a new notebook for your PSOE study materials  
  Example name: `PSOE – Study Guide`

**Goal:** Have a central hub for all your study materials.

---

## Step 2: Upload Your Study Materials

Add all relevant resources:
- PDFs, DOCs, or text files  
- Your own notes  
- Official references or links  

**Tip:** NotebookLM answers questions based on what you provide — your materials only.

**Goal:** Your study guide is based on your own trusted content.

---

## Step 3: Summarize Materials with NotebookLM

Ask NotebookLM to:
- Summarize key topics  
- Generate a high-level outline  
- Organize notes into sections  

**Outcome:** A rough table of contents for your study guide.

---

## Step 4: Build Study Guide Sections

For each topic:
- Ask NotebookLM to create:
  - Concise study notes  
  - Key points  
  - Bullet-point summaries  
  - Revision questions  

**Example prompts:**


**Outcome:** Sections with clear summaries, key concepts, and revision prompts.

---

## Step 5: Use NotebookLM for Active Revision

Interact with your study guide:
- “Quiz me on this content”  
- “What are my weak areas?”  
- “Give me 5 practice questions”  
- “Explain this topic in simpler terms”

**Goal:** Active recall and self-testing to reinforce learning.

---

## Step 6: Refine and Improve Your Study Guide

- Add more sources as you progress  
- Re-run summaries and reorganize sections  
- Remove unclear notes  
- Continuously improve the guide

**Goal:** An evolving, high-quality personal reference.

---

## Step 7: Complete the Challenge / Lab

Demonstrate that you can:
- Set up a NotebookLM notebook  
- Upload sources  
- Generate summaries and structure a study guide  
- Use NotebookLM for active revision  

**Outcome:** Earn the course badge and prove you can use NotebookLM effectively.

---

## 🧠 The Big Idea

This course teaches you **how to learn smarter, not harder**.  
NotebookLM becomes your **AI study assistant**, helping you:
- Organize materials  
- Summarize content  
- Test understanding  
- Build a living study guide  

You end up with a reusable workflow for any certification.

---

## ✅ Final Result You Should Have

By the end of the course, you should have:

- A fully set up NotebookLM notebook  
- Organized study materials inside it  
- A structured study guide  
- A workflow for summarizing and revising  
- A repeatable method for future certifications
